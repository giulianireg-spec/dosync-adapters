DoSync privacy policy

Effective: November 2026. · [Español](https://giulianireg-spec.github.io/dosync-adapters/privacidad/) · [Português](pt.html) · [Français](fr.html) · [Deutsch](de.html) · [Italiano](it.html)

DoSync is an app to control the devices in your home from your phone. It is designed so that your data stays on your phone.

What data it handles and where it stays

If you don't type the Nuki Bridge IP, the app asks Nuki's discovery service (api.nuki.io) for it, which only sees your home's internet address.

DoSync has no servers of its own that receive your data, creates no accounts, and does not sell or share information.

Local network and Bluetooth

To find your devices, the app checks the addresses on your WiFi network and asks them what they are, and looks for nearby Bluetooth devices. That communication never leaves your home. The app does not use your location: to know whether you are home, it checks whether the phone is on your devices' network.

Artificial intelligence (optional)

  • Gemini Nano, on phones that have it, runs inside the phone: nothing is sent.
  • Your own cloud AI: when a command is not understood on the phone, the text of

the command and a short list of your devices (type, room, possible actions and their state; no IP addresses or keys) are sent to the provider you chose. That request is governed by the provider's privacy policy (Anthropic, Google or OpenAI) and billed to your account with them. If you ask for something from the internet (a channel's latest video, the weather), that same provider runs the search with its own search tool. Without an AI configured, nothing is sent.

Backup

If you create a backup, the app builds a text encrypted with the password you choose and you share it wherever you want. DoSync does not receive it or keep a copy; without the password it cannot be read.

Catalogs and brand sign-in

The app periodically downloads, from the public repository github.com/giulianireg-spec/dosync-adapters, a signed adapter catalog and the infrared code library. Those downloads include none of your data. When you connect a Miele account, its sign-in page returns you to a page on that same site that only shows the code for you to copy: it does not store or send it.

Advertising

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner outside the main dashboard. The app requests non-personalized ads and, where the law requires it, your consent. AdMob may use device identifiers under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Your rights

You can delete everything by uninstalling the app, or remove devices, accounts and your AI key from the app at any time. Contact: giulianireg@gmail.com.
